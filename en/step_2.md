## Build the soil probe

Create the frame that will hold the sensor screws apart at a set distance.

--- task ---

Choose an old container lid, bottle cap, ice lolly stick, or other non-conductive material that is strong enough to hold two screws. This will form your frame.

In this example, we will use an old plastic yoghurt pot.

![A yoghurt pot with a cutting line marked around it near the base.](images/probe01.png)
--- /task ---

--- task ---

Use a ruler to mark two spots on the surface of your frame, spaced around 2 centimetres apart.
![Two dots marked on the bottom of the yoghurt pot, spaced around 2 centimetres apart.](images/probe02.png)

--- /task ---

--- task ---
 
To create holes at the marked points, tighten the screws through the spots so that the tips extend evenly on the underside and the heads are slightly raised on the top.
![Two steel screws have been inserted into the base of the yoghurt pot in the positions marked before, with the tips of the screws extending evenly on the underside and the heads of the screws slightly raised on the top.](images/probe03.png){:width="300px"}

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Check that the screw heads and threads stay apart from each other. They should be separate so that no electricity can pass between them.
![The base of the yoghurt pot has been turned upside down and the screws are separate.](images/probe04.png){:width="300px"}

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Mark one screw as **Probe A** and the other as **Probe B**. 

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Remove the socket connectors from some socket-to-pin jumper wires and tape or solder the stripped ends to the screws. In the next step, you will use the pin ends to connect the screws to your breadboard.
![In the base of the yoghurt pot, a red anode wire has been taped to one screw, marked with a + sign, and a black cathode wire has been taped to the other screw, marked with a - sign.](images/probe05.png){:width="300px"}

--- /task ---

--- task ---
 
Attach a handle (for example, a stick made from a yoghurt pot, a plastic straw, or a short wooden dowel) to your frame using tape or glue, so that you can insert your new **probe** into soil safely.
![A wooden stick attached to a plastic bottle top has been secured inside the base of the yoghurt pot.](images/probe06.png){:width="300px"}

--- /task ---
