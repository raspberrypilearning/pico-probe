## Challenge

--- task ---

Add a **buzzer** to your project to give an audio warning when the soil becomes too dry.

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Add a second sensor on **GP27** and another LED, so that you can monitor multiple plants.

--- /task ---

--- task ---

Add a **photoresistor** to your project and only check the soil during the day (when you are awake) or during the night (which would be better for automatic watering).

--- /task ---

--- task ---

**Upgrade:** Use the [Touch-free tap](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/touch-free-tap){:target="_blank"} project to help you create an automatic watering system with a pump and a relay.

--- /task ---

--- task ---

**Upgrade:** Create an IoT plant sensor — add an ESP32 IoT board and send messages across the internet when your plants need water.
